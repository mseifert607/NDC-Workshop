Regime Shift Explorer

Start in low tension. Before each observation, a one-way shift to high tension may occur. Compare two environments using the same sequence.

Model assumptions

Transition probability h is constant per round; high tension is absorbing. Signals are conditionally independent given the state. Diagnosticity q = P(drone | high) = P(bird | low). The benchmark is conditional on these assumptions, not a real-world risk estimate.

New prior = previous posterior + (1 − previous posterior) × h. Posterior = prior × likelihood in high / total probability of observation.
